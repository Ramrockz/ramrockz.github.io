Pick your Cubeman

Every character has a power of their own that changes how the tower comes down.

  • DragonFireball

    Explodes on impact and blasts the cubes away.

  • RobotSelf-Destruct

    Once he lands he blinks red, then blows up, himself included.

  • WizardArcane Burst

    Sucks the cubes in, then flings them sky-high.

  • AstronautZero Gravity

    Cubes he knocks loose float up and away.

  • CyberpunkTime Freeze

    Just after impact time stands still: the cubes hang in mid-air until touched.

  • AlienAbduction

    A UFO beams up the cubes (and him) for a while, then drops it all.

  • GrandmaKnitted

    She knits the cubes together: the tower falls apart in big pieces.

  • VikingThunder

    Lightning strikes the tower and splits it down the middle.

…and 30 more: a pirate with a cannonball, a bear that shakes the ground, a clown that bounces, a cow that tips the tower over.

Straight from the game

Throw him, watch it blow, build your own tower as tall and wide as you dare, or pick one of 20+ models.

Also: a house, a truck, a ship, a plane, a train, a windmill, a robot, a piñata and more.

One finger, one throw

Draw the arc he flies: flat and fast, or high and over the top. Let go, and he's off.

Every cube is real

Hundreds of cubes, each one tumbling, bouncing and clattering on its own. No two crashes come out the same.

Win new characters

Knock off just the top, land on the summit, fly clean over it. Some characters are only won that way, others hide inside a model's cubes.
