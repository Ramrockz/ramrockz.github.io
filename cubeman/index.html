How it plays

One finger, one throw, a few hundred cubes.

  1. Draw a flight pathSwipe the arc you want him to fly: flat and fast, or high and over the top.
  2. Let goCubeman launches along your line. Draw it fast for a hard throw, slowly for a gentle one.
  3. SmashHe slams into the tower and the cubes go flying. Count what's left, then throw again.

Towers and models

Build your own tower, as tall and wide as you dare, or pick a model: a castle tower, a ship, a rocket, a lighthouse, a train, a piñata and more.

Achievements

Knock off just the top, land on the summit, fly clean over it. Dozens of challenges, and some characters are only won that way. Others hide inside a model's cubes.

Slow-motion replays

Every throw can be watched again in slow motion, from a camera that follows the crash. Like it? Share the clip.

Your own cubes

Rainbow, toy blocks, bricks, gold, neon, or a photo you take yourself, printed on every cube of the tower.
