Houses full of dirt

Every room comes run down, partied in or hoarded, and new rooms open as you clean.

Also: a flat share after the party, a hoarder's flat, a school, a china shop, a robot factory, a pyramid, a treasure vault and more.

Watch it come clean

Every wipe takes the grime off where your finger goes: dust, grease, mould and limescale, each with the tool it likes best.

Fix it up

Unblock the drains, strip the old wallpaper, fill the holes and paint the walls fresh.

Finds under the rubbish

Bin the junk and you might find treasure: sell it for coins or keep it in your collection.

No timer, no stress

Your houses stay just as you left them. Clean a little or a lot, whenever you like.
