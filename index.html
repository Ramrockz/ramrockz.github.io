Everything breaks

Walls, floors, fixtures and the smallest thing on a shelf. Nothing is just decoration.

Nothing floats

Cut what holds it up and it comes down, along with everything hanging on it.

Rewind

One tap plays it all back in reverse until the room is whole again.
